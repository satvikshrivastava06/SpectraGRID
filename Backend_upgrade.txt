My recommendation

Resist the temptation to keep polishing the frontend after the Antigravity improvements. At that point, the visual experience will already be compelling.

The highest return on your time will come from building the backend services, AI pipeline, Digital Twin engine, and deployment infrastructure. Those pieces are what transform SpectraGrid from a visually impressive prototype into a technically credible platform that can stand up in discussions with engineers, investors, or accelerator reviewers. Based on everything you've shared so far, I'd prioritize the remaining work in this order:

Backend API + database
Digital Twin data model
Ghost Generation and AI engine
DevOps and deployment pipeline
Security, testing, and documentation
