1. Holographic Glass Material

Instead of standard glassmorphism:

Create a multi-layer optical material:

Dynamic refraction
Chromatic aberration on edges
Micro reflections following cursor movement
Fresnel edge highlights
Thin-film interference effect similar to spacecraft visors

The glass should feel physically present in 3D space.

As the cursor moves:

reflections shift,
edges shimmer,
light bends through the panel.

This effect can be achieved using:

React Three Fiber
Custom GLSL shaders
Drei MeshTransmissionMaterial
Framer Motion for interpolation
