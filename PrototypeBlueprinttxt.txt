2. Core Concept: Ghost Generation Index (GGI)
Create a first-class business metric.
Ghost Generation Index
GGI = Expected Energy - Actual Energy
Where:
Expected Energy =
f(
irradiance,
temperature,
panel age,
historical efficiency,
season,
soiling factor,
shading factor
)
Example:
Metric	Value
Expected output	138 kWh
Actual output	104 kWh
Ghost generation	34 kWh
Financial loss	₹272
Carbon impact	24 kg CO₂
This becomes the platform's primary KPI.

What happened?

What is happening?

Renewable Asset Operating System
